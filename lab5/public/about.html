AURA was founded on a simple principle: athletic apparel should eliminate distractions, maximize output, and endure the harshest training regimens on earth.

Our Story

Obsessive Engineering Behind Every Stitch

Founded in 2022 in Boulder, Colorado, AURA Athletics started in an underground garage where marathoners, weightlifters, and fabric scientists gathered with a shared frustration: performance wear that degraded after a single season.

We threw out conventional polyester blends and rebuilt apparel from molecular yarn up. Today, we work with Olympic sprinters, CrossFit champions, and ultra-endurance runners across 40 countries to test every prototype to failure before it hits the rack.
