ChatGPT is an artificial intelligence chatbot developed by OpenAI. It can understand and generate human-like text, answer questions, explain concepts, help with coding, write and summarize content, translate languages, and assist with many everyday tasks. It uses advanced language models to understand the context of a conversation and generate useful responses. ChatGPT can be used by students, developers, professionals, and businesses for learning, problem-solving, research, brainstorming, and productivity.
